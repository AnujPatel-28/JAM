# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in JAM welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give context generously.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's information.
- Prioritize the health of the community and the safety of users.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed in the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently ban participation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
